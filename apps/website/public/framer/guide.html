THE SETUP GUIDE

A little winter.
A few simple steps.

The Framer edition is being prepared for release. The final component link and checkout are not available yet.

Add Winter

  1. Once available, insert Winter using the component URL supplied with your purchase.
  2. Place one instance on each page that needs snow. Set the layer to Absolute positioning so its editor marker does not reserve space in a Stack.
  3. Choose Whole page coverage, or place Winter directly inside the desired section and choose Parent frame.
  4. Choose an atmosphere and adjust the visual controls.
  5. Open Preview, then check your mobile breakpoint, links and navigation before publishing.

The canvas marker is static. The snow runs in Preview and on the published page.

Your forecast, your way.

Snowfall & Mobile snow
Set desktop density, then its mobile multiplier below 768px.
Snow color, flakes & wind
Choose the appearance and direction. Gentle, Alpine, Blizzard and Minimal are the starting atmospheres.
Settling & Max depth
Let snow collect on supported elements and limit its depth.
Start dusting
Add already-settled snow on startup. Set to 0 to let snowfall build the scene entirely over time. Restart the component to apply a new initial dusting.
Brush & Snowballs
Brush settled snow with your pointer. Optional desktop snowball gathering uses Shift + press and drag. Touch scrolling remains available.
Pause button
Give visitors control of the effect. Reduced-motion preferences are always respected.

A place to land.

Buttons, links, images, articles and elements with the card class are detected automatically. Large headings and whole sections are deliberately omitted to keep display typography clean. For deliberate Framer frames, try the layer name Snow surface. To exclude a frame and its descendants, try No snow. Confirm that the published page preserves those layer-name attributes.

Snow uses browser geometry approximations. Complex 3D transforms, arbitrary masks, iframe contents and every stacking arrangement cannot be reconstructed exactly. Parent-frame coverage uses a rectangular clipping approximation.

Keep things considerate.

Use modest snowfall on smaller screens. Keep the pause button available. The component always respects reduced motion and does not replace the visitor’s cursor.

The integration loads a version-pinned free MIT engine from Fluri’s HTTPS host. Self-hosting instructions and license notices are included in the product package. It does not send interaction telemetry.

Already using the free engine?

The npm engine remains free under MIT, including commercial use. The Framer edition packages the original adapter, visual controls and setup experience around it.
