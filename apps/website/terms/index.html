Report totals responsibly

Reporting is available only after domain verification and an explicit reporting choice. Send daily aggregate effect-impression totals and the reported Fluri version from a backend you control. Keep reporting credentials out of browser code, public repositories, logs, and URLs. A new reporting key revokes the old key.

Do not submit individual events, visitor identifiers, IP addresses, fingerprints, personal page URLs, or other visitor-level data. Send only counts collected and shared lawfully, with required notices, permissions, and visitor consent where applicable. A cookie-free implementation or our acceptance of a report does not establish a legal exemption. Check your own jurisdiction and measurement setup before collecting or sending statistics.

Reports are owner-supplied and may be incomplete or inaccurate. They measure reported impressions, not unique people or all Fluri installations. Do not fabricate or inflate counts.
